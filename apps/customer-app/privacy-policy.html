Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At Next360, accessible from our mobile applications and web services, protecting your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect and how we use it to provide organic marketplace and delivery services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, email address, and delivery addresses provided during registration or checkout.
  • Location Data: Device location accessed with your permission to identify delivery zones and nearby organic vendor stores.
  • Order & Transaction History: Purchase history, wallet balances, and support tickets.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and push notification tokens for order updates.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Fulfill orders and process payments via Razorpay
  • Provide real-time delivery tracking
  • Send critical order status notifications
  • Improve our services and user experience
  • Prevent fraud and ensure platform security

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with:

  • Vendors: Order details necessary for fulfillment (name, delivery address, items ordered)
  • Delivery Partners: Delivery address and contact info for order delivery
  • Payment Processor (Razorpay): Transaction data for payment processing
  • Government: When required by law or legal process

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (TLS), secure token-based authentication, and regular security audits. Your payment information is processed securely through Razorpay and is never stored on our servers.

5. Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account and personal data at any time. You can:

  • Submit a deletion request in the app under Profile > Delete Account & Data
  • Email us at support@next360.com

We will process your deletion request within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Next360 is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
