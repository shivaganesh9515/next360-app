**Full description (≤ 4,000 chars)**
```
Fresh groceries, the honest way.

Next360 brings you three carefully curated storefronts in one app:

🍃 ORGANIC — Certified organic produce, grains and staples sourced straight from local farmers.
🌿 NATURAL — Minimally processed foods and everyday essentials, with nothing artificial added.
🌏 ECO-FRIENDLY — Sustainable, low-waste products for a home that treads a little lighter.

Why Next360
• Farm-direct sourcing — support local growers with every order
• OTP login — no passwords, just your phone number
• Fast delivery across Hyderabad & Vijayawada
• Live order tracking with map view and delivery OTP
• Pay your way — Cash on Delivery (up to ₹2,000) or secure online payments via Razorpay

• Shop in English or Telugu

1. Log in with your mobile number
2. Choose your delivery location
3. Shop any of the three storefronts
4. Checkout with COD or online payment
5. Track your order live until it arrives

Every order is packed fresh, fulfilled by local vendors and verified by our team. Damaged, wrong or expired items? Request a return from My Orders within 24 hours of delivery.

Download Next360 today and eat clean, naturally and eco-friendly.
